Work

MouthPad^

A new human-computer interface controlled by the tongue.

MouthPad^ turns subtle tongue movements into precise control of phones, computers and other digital devices. It gives people a way to interact with technology when hands are not an option.

  • Cannes Lions, Grand Prix, Innovation
  • Cannes Lions, Silver, Health & Wellness
  • Cannes Lions, Shortlist, Titanium
  • CES Innovation Awards, Honoree, Accessibility

Vanishing Emails

A system that makes promotional emails disappear when they expire.

Vanishing Emails turned an invisible source of digital waste into a simple action. Once an offer was no longer useful, the email deleted itself.

  • Cannes Lions, Bronze, Innovation
  • Cannes Lions, Shortlist, Titanium
  • El Ojo, Silver, Innovation
  • El Ojo, Silver, Digital & Social

Bank on Equality

A banking action that made the gender pay gap visible through money.

Bank on Equality brought the pay gap into the bank’s own products and transactions. It turned an abstract inequality into a financial benefit for women.

  • Cannes Lions, Bronze, PR
  • El Ojo, Best Idea, Peru
  • Clio, Silver, Public Relations
  • Clio, Bronze, Corporate Image
  • El Ojo, Gold, Direct

Hey! He Is Not a Kid

A stadium intervention that exposed how easily adults can impersonate children online.

Hey! He Is Not a Kid brought that digital threat into a public space. The deception became visible and impossible to ignore.

  • Clio Sports, Gold, Social Good
  • Clio Sports, Silver, Gameday / In-Game Experience
  • New York Festivals, Gold, PR
  • El Ojo, Bronze, Sport

En One

A real-time payment feature that gave Peru’s micro-merchants immediate access to their money.

With En One, digital payments became available instantly. Before, merchants had to wait 24 hours or more to use those funds.

  • Effie Peru, Gold, Marketing Innovation
  • Effie Peru, Silver, Business to Business

Method

I start by looking at what tends to go unnoticed in a brief, brand or technology. It may be a behavior, an unused capability or a tension no one has named yet.

From there, I define a direction and decide what it should become: a campaign, product, interface or experience.

AI expands and deepens what I can explore. Creative judgment decides what is worth developing.
