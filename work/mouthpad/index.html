MouthPad^

Work / Innovation

A new human-computer interface controlled by the tongue.

The overlooked opportunity

Most digital interfaces are built around a simple assumption: hands are available. When they aren’t, the interface itself can become the barrier.

The idea

MouthPad^ turns subtle tongue movements into precise control of phones, computers and other digital devices. It gives people a way to interact with technology when hands are not an option.

The shift was simple: instead of adapting another hand-based controller, turn an overlooked part of the body into the interface.

What became real

A hands-free control system built around the mouth as a precise input surface. The idea moved from an interface proposition into something people could actually use with everyday digital devices.
