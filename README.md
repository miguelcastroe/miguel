# Miguel Castro Portfolio

Miguel Castro is a Creative Director working from what others miss.

This portfolio presents selected work across creativity, technology, culture and AI, with a focus on ideas that reveal overlooked tensions, hidden behaviors and unspoken contradictions. The work moves between innovation, accessibility, social impact, digital products, cultural interventions and AI-assisted creative thinking.

Miguel has been recognized at Cannes Lions, including a Grand Prix in Innovation and a Bronze in Innovation, along with international recognition across creativity, health, wellness, PR, innovation, digital, social impact and technology-led work.

## Selected work

### MouthPad^

MouthPad^ is a mouth interface for hands-free digital control. It reframes digital access through the body, creating a new way for people with limited mobility to interact with technology.

Recognition includes Cannes Lions Grand Prix in Innovation, Cannes Lions Silver in Health & Wellness, Cannes Lions Titanium Shortlist and CES Innovation Awards recognition in Accessibility and AgeTech.

### Vanishing Emails

Vanishing Emails is a tool that makes expired promotional emails disappear. It turns an overlooked digital behavior into an environmental idea by addressing the hidden footprint of promotional email storage.

Recognition includes Cannes Lions Bronze in Innovation, Cannes Lions Titanium Shortlist, El Ojo Silver in Innovation and El Ojo Silver in Digital & Social.

### Bank on Equality

Bank on Equality is a banking action that returned value equivalent to the wage gap. It reframed the wage gap not only as a statistic, but as missing financial value.

Recognition includes Cannes Lions Bronze in PR, Clio Silver in Public Relations and multiple El Ojo awards.

### Hey! He Is Not a Kid

Hey! He Is Not a Kid is a football intervention exposing online identity deception and the risks of grooming hidden behind false trust.

Recognition includes Clio Sports Gold, Clio Sports Silver, New York Festivals Gold and El Ojo recognition.

### IRBIN

IRBIN is an intelligent recycling object that helps people classify waste at the exact moment where hesitation, guessing and failure usually happen.

Recognition includes El Ojo Innovation Bronze.

### Behind The Fear

Behind The Fear is a hidden-message mechanism inside email, designed to reveal distress signals from victims of domestic violence through a channel built for ordinary communication.

Recognition includes El Ojo Innovation Bronze.

## Essay on AI

The portfolio also includes the essay **MLM: Judgment Before Output**, a long-form piece about creative judgment in the age of generative AI.

The essay explores the risk of finished-looking outputs arriving before human judgment has had enough time to form. It presents Miguel's Language Model as a human-centered framework for protecting the interval before output becomes persuasive enough to trust.

The essay connects AI, creative practice, automation, fluency, craft, expertise and authorship. It positions AI not as a replacement for human intuition, but as a tool that requires stronger timing, sharper judgment and more deliberate creative operations.

## Core positioning

Miguel Castro works with the material before it becomes too polished to question.

His value is not more output. It is better judgment before the work goes out.

He helps identify the hidden tension inside a brief, product, technology, campaign, deck or creative direction, then shapes it into something people can use, see or feel.

Bring him in when the idea is promising, but not yet clear enough to survive reality.
